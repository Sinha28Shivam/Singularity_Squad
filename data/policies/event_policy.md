# Workplace Event Policy (SYNTHETIC - for demo only)

## EVT-2.2 Lead time for event requests
Employee event requests that involve external catering must be submitted at least 5 business days before the event date. Requests with shorter lead time require written approval from the Department Head and are flagged for human review.

## EVT-2.4 Large gatherings
Events with more than 150 attendees require a Facilities review for space, safety, and fire-code capacity before any vendor is booked.

## EVT-3.1 Per-head catering cap
Catering for employee events must not exceed INR 900 per person (before taxes and delivery fees). Vendors whose per-person rate exceeds this cap must not be selected without CFO exception approval.

## EVT-3.3 Budget ceiling
The total estimated cost of an event, including taxes and delivery or setup fees, must not exceed the budget limit stated in the request. If the estimate exceeds the budget, the request must be revised or escalated; it must not proceed to a purchase order.

## EVT-4.1 Dietary inclusivity
Catering menus should include vegetarian options and clearly label common allergens.
