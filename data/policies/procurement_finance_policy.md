# Procurement & Finance Policy (SYNTHETIC - for demo only)

## PROC-2.1 Approved vendors only
Goods and services for workplace events must be purchased only from vendors on the approved vendor list. Non-approved vendors must not be used, regardless of price.

## PROC-3.2 Vendor selection
When several approved vendors are eligible, prefer the designated preferred vendor; otherwise choose the highest-rated eligible vendor, using lower total cost as a tie-breaker. Record the reason for the selection.

## PROC-4.1 Approval thresholds
Purchase orders up to INR 50,000 require Team Manager approval. Purchase orders above INR 50,000 and up to INR 1,50,000 require approval by the Department Head and the Finance Business Partner. Purchase orders above INR 1,50,000 require CFO approval. All system-generated purchase orders are drafts until a human approver signs off.

## FIN-5.1 Three-way match
Before payment, every invoice must be matched against the purchase order and the delivery record (three-way match). Vendor ID and PO number must match.

## FIN-5.2 Delivery evidence
Billed quantities must not exceed quantities confirmed in a signed delivery record. Any shortfall must be disputed with the vendor before payment.

## FIN-5.3 Amount tolerance
Invoice totals may differ from the expected total by no more than INR 1.00 (rounding). Any larger variance, or any unit-price difference from the PO, is a mismatch requiring Finance review.

## FIN-5.4 Duplicate invoices
An invoice number that has previously been submitted or paid by the same vendor must be rejected as a potential duplicate and escalated to Finance.
